Model credits

The characters' anatomical head mesh is adapted from Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite-Realities, based on work at Triple Gangers.

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported. Original distribution: Three.js r180 LeePerrySmith example assets.

Harborlight changes: cropped the bust to the head and upper neck, reduced the triangle count, rescaled it to the game's metre convention, adjusted depth, opened the eye regions and added eyes, blinking lids, local skin colors and fitted hair. Bodies, clothes, hands and activity poses are authored for Harborlight. No photographic face textures or runtime asset downloads are used.
